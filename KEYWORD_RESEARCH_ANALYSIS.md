## 🎯 AI SEARCH RANKING OPTIMIZATION ROADMAP

### Google SGE (Search Generative Experience) Optimization

**Keywords SGE Favors:**
- "Best [product] for [industry]" - Include in content
- How-to and step-by-step guides
- Comparison content (vs MasterSoft, vs Camu)
- Data-backed statistics
- Original research
- Expert opinions

**Content Optimization for SGE:**
```
DO:
✅ Include factual data points
✅ Use clear headings & subheadings
✅ Create listicles (top 10, best 5, etc.)
✅ Include expert quotes
✅ Add statistics & research
✅ Define technical terms
✅ Answer common questions

DON'T:
❌ Thin content (<1000 words)
❌ Duplicate content across pages
❌ Keyword stuffing
❌ Unverified claims
❌ Poor grammar/formatting
```
