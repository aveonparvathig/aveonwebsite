## RISK MITIGATION

### Major Risks & Solutions

**Risk 1: Low University Response Rates**
- Solution: Highly personalize outreach, use referrals when possible, multiple contact attempts, offer genuine value

**Risk 2: Publication Rejections**
- Solution: Have 5-10 article angles ready, target lower-DA publications first, consider paid placement as backup

**Risk 3: Spam Links Not Impacting**
- Solution: Track DA 4 weeks post-disavow, focus on building quality links while cleaning, re-evaluate if needed

**Risk 4: Slow DA Improvement**
- Solution: Track referring domains immediately (faster indicator), focus on consistent acquisition, have patience (normal pattern)

**Risk 5: New Spam Backlinks**
- Solution: Monthly monitoring, quick disavow of new spam, avoid engaging with low-quality sites, keep disavow updated
