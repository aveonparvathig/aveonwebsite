What page one does that Aveon's page does not:
1. **Says "college ERP software" and "college management software" in the title, H1 and body.** Aveon's H1 is the brand name ("Aveon College Management System") and the body never uses "ERP software".

## 5. Roundup page proposal (not built: needs approval)

Two of the top five results are competitor-written "Top 10 college ERP software in India" articles. A factual roundup on aveoninfotech.com would compete directly, but it has to name competitors and describe them. Earlier in this project the decision was that `/comparisons` makes no claims about named vendors. Options:

- **A. Factual roundup, sourced**: "Best College ERP Software in India (2026): 10 vendors compared on affiliation support, NAAC reporting and deployment", every competitor line drawn only from their own public pages with a link, dated, and with Aveon disclosed as the author. This is what Edumerge and VAPS do and it ranks.
- **B. Stay neutral**: keep the scorecard, do not publish competitor descriptions, accept that the roundup SERP stays with competitors.

Recommendation: A, with the rule that every competitor sentence quotes or links to the vendor's own page. Say "build the roundup" to proceed.

## 6. What decides the national ranking

On-page gaps above are worth roughly positions 50 → 20. Page one for "college erp software" is held by sites with hundreds of referring domains; Aveon has close to none. The Phase 3 off-site plan (client-portal footers once cleaned, SoftwareSuggest/G2/GoodFirms/Capterra, monthly guest article, Wikidata/Crunchbase) is the lever for the last 20 positions. Regional terms (Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu) are already page one and should be defended first.
