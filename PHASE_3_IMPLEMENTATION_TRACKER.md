## MASTER BACKLINK TRACKING TEMPLATE

**Create in Google Sheets or Excel**

### Column Headers:

```
A: Date Acquired
B: Link Source (Company/Publication Name)
C: Source URL (Where the link is)
D: Target URL (What page on your site)
E: Domain Authority
F: Domain Rating (Ahrefs)
G: Domain Authority MOZ (if different)
H: Anchor Text
I: Link Type (Follow/No-Follow)
J: Relevance Category (edu/gov/publication/directory/other)
K: Relevance Score (1-40)
L: Content Quality (1-20)
M: Placement Quality (1-10)
N: Anchor Quality (1-20)
O: TOTAL Quality Score (J+K+L+M+N)
P: Status (Live/Processing/Dead/Pending)
Q: Indexed in GSC (Yes/No/Pending)
R: Outreach Method (Email/Direct/Referral/etc)
S: Contact Person
T: Contact Email
U: Response Time (Days)
V: Conversion Rate (1st/2nd/3rd attempt)
W: Notes
X: Link Health (Last checked date)
Y: Issues/Follow-ups
```

### Example Entry:

```
A: 10/7/2026

E: 95
F: 92
G: 93
H: campus ERP solutions
I: Follow
J: edu
K: 40
L: 18
M: 10
N: 18
O: 86 (Excellent)
P: Live
Q: Yes
R: Email
S: John Smith
T: john@stanford.edu
U: 3
V: 1st attempt
W: Case study collaboration
X: 10/7/2026
Y: None
```

## NOTES FOR EACH TEMPLATE

1. **Master Backlink Tracker:** Update weekly with new links. Use formulas to calculate total quality score.
2. **Outreach Trackers:** Update after every email sent. Track conversion rates by week.
3. **Monthly Dashboard:** Fill out on last day of each month. Share with leadership.
4. **Weekly Status:** Fill every Friday (30 minutes). Use to catch issues early.
5. **DA Trend:** Check every 2 weeks manually via Google Search Console.
6. **Link Health:** Run monthly to catch dead links early.
7. **Daily Activity Log:** Keep simple, 5-10 minute daily update.
8. **Competitor Analysis:** Review quarterly to adjust strategy.
9. **ROI Calculation:** Calculate at end of Month 3 and Month 6.
