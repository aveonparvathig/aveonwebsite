This is the **BEST practice** used by:

## 🎯 THE STRATEGY

### **Main Navigation (STAYS CLEAN)**
```
Home | Products | Services | Solutions | Company | Contact
```

```

/naac/                                   ← Users click links to it
/services/software-development/          ← Internal links lead there
/services/warehouse-management/          ← Organic discovery
/products/coe/                           ← Product sub-pages
[... unlimited pages without menu bloat]
```

### **Discovery Flow (Users FIND Them)**
```
1. Google Search: "COE examination management"
   ↓
   Lands on: /coe/ (no menu navigation needed)

2. Blog Post: "NAAC Accreditation Tips"
   ↓
   Link: "Automate NAAC reporting →"
   ↓
   Lands on: /naac/ (from link, not menu)

3. Product Page: "Examination Management"
   ↓
   Related: "Our COE Solution →"
   ↓
   Lands on: /coe/ (from internal link)
```
