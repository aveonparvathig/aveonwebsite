## STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

**The Strategy:** Create content so valuable that it naturally attracts backlinks while establishing authority.

**Content Pillars drive Backlinks:**
1. Blog post published on your site → attracts internal links + social shares
2. Content repurposed as guest article → placed on high-DA publication (backlink)
3. Data/research becomes citable → publications link to your research
4. Webinar content creates authority → speakers/attendees link to event
5. Original research becomes reference → cited across education sector

**This creates a flywheel:** Content → Backlinks → Authority → More Content Opportunities
