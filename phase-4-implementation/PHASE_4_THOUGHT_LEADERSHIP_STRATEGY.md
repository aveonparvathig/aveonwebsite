### 1.3 Blog Post Template & Quality Standards

**Every blog post must include:**
- Headline: 50-60 characters, keyword-rich
- Meta description: 155-160 characters
- Word count: 3,000-5,000 (comprehensive)
- Internal links: 8-12 (to other content)
- External links: 3-5 (to authoritative sources)
- Call-to-action: Lead capture or demo request
- Author bio: 50-100 words with credentials
- Date published: Use actual publish date
- Update date: When refreshed (critical for SEO)
- Schema markup: Article, FAQ, BreadcrumbList
- Images: 3-4 optimized images per post
- Subheadings: H2, H3, H4 structure (scannable)
- Key statistics: Data-driven insights (citable)

**SEO Checklist Before Publish:**
- [ ] Keyword research (primary + 5 LSI keywords)
- [ ] Competitor content reviewed (know what's out there)
- [ ] Target keyword in: title, meta, H1, first 100 words, internal links
- [ ] Reading level: 8th grade (accessibility)
- [ ] Readability: Short sentences, varied length
- [ ] Engagement: Questions, examples, case studies
- [ ] Links: All work, relevant, recent
- [ ] Mobile preview: Tested on phone
- [ ] Canonical: Set if republished elsewhere
- [ ] Author: Verified with social links

## SECTION 9: COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

**❌ DON'T:**
1. Write generic listicles ("5 Tips...") without research
2. Publish without proofreading (damages credibility)
3. Neglect internal linking (missed SEO opportunity)
4. Forget calls-to-action (waste of traffic)
5. Update old posts without marking date (stale content penalty)
6. Pitch same article idea to multiple publications (damage relationships)
7. Host webinar with no follow-up plan (waste of leads)
8. Ignore comments/questions (kills engagement)
9. Create content without distribution plan (shouting into void)
10. Steal ideas/data without attribution (ethical & SEO risk)

**✅ DO:**
1. Base content on original data & research
2. Proofread 3 times (skim, read, skim again)
3. Link to 8-12 internal posts per article
4. Always include lead capture (demo, guide, webinar)
5. Update old posts quarterly + mark publication date
6. Customize pitches to each publication's voice
7. Follow up with every webinar attendee
8. Respond to every comment within 24 hours
9. Create distribution plan before writing
10. Cite sources & link to original research
