## COMMON QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

**Q: What if a guest article pitch gets rejected?**
A: Don't take it personally. Customize and pitch to next publication. Expect 30% acceptance rate.

**Q: How do I ensure blog posts rank in Google?**
A: Follow SEO checklist in Phase 4 strategy. Link to authoritative sources, use data, update regularly.

**Q: Should I hire expensive writers or use AI?**
A: Start with human writers for credibility. Once you have templates, mix in AI (Claude, ChatGPT) for drafts you then edit.

**Q: What if we can't get 50 survey responses?**
A: Start with 20-30 responses and publish preliminary findings. Add "Survey ongoing - responses welcome."

**Q: Can we do this with less budget?**
A: Yes. Do it yourself + hire 1 writer, not 2. Use free webinar tools. Write some articles yourself.
