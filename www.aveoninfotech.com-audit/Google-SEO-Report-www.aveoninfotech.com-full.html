1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of www.aveoninfotech.com for a B2B SaaS — education / campus ERP (Coimbatore HQ, India-wide) site, generated on November 16, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

81/100
SEO Health Score
Critical Issues Found:
  1. Off-site authority: 2 referring hosts vs 28 for academiaerp.com
  2. Old pages contradict confirmed facts (years, students, modules) and stale public/ai-* files
  3. Doubtful trust claims: Symbiosis case study, 2024 publish dates, ISO 9001 line
  4. Comparison pages not yet discovered by Google; orphan pages; broken Coimbatore hero image
  5. No client proof or product visuals on product and ad landing pages
Quick Wins:
  1. Resubmit sitemap and request indexing for the five comparison pages; add them to llms.txt
  2. Correct contradictory stats and delete stale public/ai-* files
  3. Mark GA4 key events
  4. Fix orphan links and the Coimbatore hero image path
  5. Shorten >60-char titles with title.absolute

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 93/100

What Works

  • All 66 sitemap URLs 200, self-canonical, indexable
  • Legacy .php and host variants 308 in one hop
  • Server-rendered; IndexNow live
  • /get-started landing pages noindex and outside sitemap

Findings

Three state slugs 404 instead of 308 Medium

college-erp-kerala, -andhra-pradesh, -uttar-pradesh return 404 (guessed slugs)

Recommendation: Add 308s only if they were ever linked or indexed

Drift on /products/college-erp and /services Medium

H1/title changed since baseline (deliberate retargeting)

Recommendation: Watch GSC two weeks, then re-baseline

CSP report-only and weak Low

unsafe-inline/unsafe-eval in script-src

Recommendation: Move to nonce-based CSP and enforce

messtoken subdomain indexable Low

index,follow on messtoken.aveoninfotech.com

Recommendation: noindex at infrastructure level
