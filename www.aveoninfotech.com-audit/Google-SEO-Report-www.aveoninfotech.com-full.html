1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of www.aveoninfotech.com for a B2B SaaS — Education / Campus ERP & LMS (with local-business HQ in Coimbatore, India) site, generated on November 06, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

84/100
SEO Health Score
Critical Issues Found:
  1. Near-zero off-site authority: only 2 referring hosts in Common Crawl; domain PageRank rank ~52 millionth — the single biggest growth blocker.
  2. 98% of clicks are branded ('aveon', 'aveon infotech', 'aveon erp'); non-branded impressions convert ZERO clicks.
  3. Non-branded category terms (college erp software, campus management system) sit on page 2+ or barely register.
  4. Technical SEO, schema and Core Web Vitals (field) are genuinely excellent — the site is well-built but commercially invisible for non-brand demand.
  5. Several thin pages (/partners 124w, /academy 232w, /solutions/by-state 225w) and Organization-only blog authorship weaken E-E-A-T.
Quick Wins:
  1. Add a named Person author (with bio + credentials) to blog Article schema for E-E-A-T.
  2. Add Content-Security-Policy header (all other security headers already present).
  3. Enrich the thin pages (/partners, /academy, /solutions/by-state) above ~500 words.
  4. Publish llms.txt and consider Content-Signal lines (optional AI-search hygiene).
  5. Add FAQ + SoftwareApplication/breadcrumb schema to solutions and NAAC/NBA sub-pages that currently lack them.

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 90/100

What Works

  • robots.txt clean: Allow /, blocks only /api/ and /studio/, declares deliberate groups for all major AI crawlers.
  • Valid sitemap.xml with 59 URLs, submitted to GSC, 0 warnings / 0 errors.
  • Legacy .php URLs 308-redirect to new Next.js routes (e.g. /college-erp.php -> /products/college-erp).
  • Security headers present: HSTS (2yr), X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy.
  • Server-rendered HTML (2,000+ words without JS); canonicals self-referential and matching Google's chosen canonical.
  • Key pages 'Submitted and indexed' in GSC with breadcrumb rich results passing.

Findings

Legacy .php URLs still out-ranking their new targets Medium

/college-erp.php pulls 1,479 impressions at pos 4.7 while its 308 target /products/college-erp gets 427 impressions at pos 6.9. Redirects are correct; Google simply hasn't finished consolidating equity to the new URLs.

Recommendation: Keep the 308s, resubmit new URLs via the Indexing API, and ensure every internal link points to the new path. Monitor until legacy URLs drop from the index.

No Content-Security-Policy header Low

All other recommended security headers are present; CSP is the one gap.

Recommendation: Add a CSP header (report-only first) via Vercel/next.config headers().

App subdomains appear in search (messtoken.aveoninfotech.com) Low

Client/app subdomains surface in GSC results for client-portal login queries; harmless but dilutes brand SERP.

Recommendation: Confirm app subdomains are noindex where appropriate and not competing with marketing pages.

2.2 Content Quality

Score: 72/100

What Works

  • Money pages are substantial: /products/college-erp ~2,593 words, /products/university-erp ~1,973 words, /comparisons ~1,500 words.
  • 12 topical blog guides (NAAC, NBA, AICTE, NIRF, OBE, ERP migration) targeting real buyer questions.
  • FAQ content present and marked up on product, contact and accreditation pages.

Findings

Blog authorship is Organization, not a credentialed Person Medium

Article schema uses author @type Organization. For E-E-A-T in a YMYL-adjacent B2B niche, named experts with bios and credentials are far stronger.

Recommendation: Add Person author entities with bio, role and sameAs (LinkedIn) to each guide; show author boxes on-page.

Thin pages Medium

/partners ~124 words, /academy ~232 words, /solutions/by-state ~225 words, /solutions/coimbatore ~351 words. Thin pages dilute quality signals and rarely rank.

Recommendation: Expand each past ~500-700 words with unique, useful content; make /solutions/by-state a real hub with descriptive link context.

Blog content batch-published same date Low

Multiple guides share datePublished = dateModified = 2026-11-03, a scaled-content pattern.

Recommendation: Stagger updates, add original data/screenshots/case detail so pages read as genuinely maintained.

2.3 On-Page SEO

Score: 85/100

What Works

  • Unique, keyword-aligned titles and meta descriptions per page; homepage title 'Campus ERP & LMS for Universities & Colleges | Aveon Infotech'.
  • Clear heading hierarchy and descriptive internal linking between related products.
  • og:/twitter: tags and theme-color present; canonical correct.

Findings

Legacy meta keywords tag in use Low

A long meta keywords tag is present (deprecated, ignored by Google; harmless but signals dated SEO).

Recommendation: Remove meta keywords; redirect effort to body copy and internal anchors.

Non-branded intent under-served by internal anchors Medium

Category head terms (college erp software, campus management system) rank page 2+. Internal anchor text and hub structure under-reinforce these.

Recommendation: Build a topic-cluster internal-link model: product pages as money pages, blog guides linking up with exact-match/partial anchors.

2.4 Schema / Structured Data

Score: 95/100

What Works

  • Sitewide Organization + LocalBusiness + WebSite JSON-LD, valid and consistent.
  • Product pages carry SoftwareApplication + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList.
  • Blog carries Article + WebPage + BreadcrumbList with dates.
  • GSC rich-results verdict PASS (breadcrumbs detected).

Findings

Schema uneven across page types Low

/solutions/* pages lack FAQ/SoftwareApplication; /products/iqac-naac-nba/naac has FAQ but no BreadcrumbList.

Recommendation: Standardise: Breadcrumb on every deep page, FAQ where Q&A exists, SoftwareApplication on every product/solution page.

No aggregateRating / review schema despite real GBP reviews Medium

Business has 4.2 stars / 54 Google reviews but no Review/AggregateRating markup is exposed.

Recommendation: Add AggregateRating (sourced legitimately) to Organization/SoftwareApplication to earn review rich results.

2.6 AI Search Readiness (GEO)

Score: 80/100

What Works

  • robots.txt explicitly welcomes GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, etc.
  • Server-rendered content readable by non-JS fetchers; rich entity schema aids extraction.
  • FAQ/Q&A structure supports passage-level citation.

Findings

No off-site brand authority for AI engines to cite High

AI answer engines favour entities with third-party corroboration. With ~2 referring hosts and branded-only presence, the brand lacks the external footprint needed for non-branded AI citations.

Recommendation: Earn directory listings, review-site profiles (G2/Capterra/SoftwareSuggest), guest articles and press — the same lever that fixes organic authority.

No llms.txt / Content-Signal / Markdown delivery Low

Optional AI-discovery hygiene not implemented (none are ranking factors today).

Recommendation: Publish /llms.txt summarising key pages; optionally add Content-Signal lines to robots groups.

2.7 Off-Site Authority & Backlinks

Score: 35/100

What Works

  • Domain is present in Common Crawl and in the ranking graph (not sandboxed/penalised).

Findings

Near-zero backlink profile Critical

Only 2 referring hosts in the Common Crawl web graph; domain PageRank rank ~51,994,670 (harmonic-centrality rank ~10.9M). This is the primary reason non-branded commercial terms do not rank and traffic is 98% branded.

Recommendation: Launch a sustained link/citation program: software review sites (G2, Capterra, SoftwareSuggest, TrustRadius), Indian edtech directories, client case-study backlinks, HARO/press, and partner co-marketing. Target 20-30 quality referring domains over 2-3 months.

No measurable authority flow to new URLs High

Equity from legacy .php pages and any historical links isn't translating into non-branded rankings.

Recommendation: Pair link-building with the internal topic-cluster model so new authority concentrates on money pages.

3. Action Plan

Phase 1: Critical — Build Off-Site Authority Weeks 1-4 (start now, runs ongoing)

  • Claim/complete G2, Capterra, SoftwareSuggest, TrustRadius profiles (do-follow + citation value).
  • List in Indian edtech / SaaS directories and local Coimbatore business directories (consistent NAP).
  • Turn existing clients into case-study backlinks (link from their sites / press).
  • Pitch 3-5 guest articles / expert quotes (HARO-style) in Indian education & edtech media.

Phase 2: High-Impact On-Page & E-E-A-T Weeks 2-3

  • Add named Person authors with bios + sameAs to all blog Article schema and on-page author boxes.
  • Expand thin pages (/partners, /academy, /solutions/by-state, /solutions/coimbatore) past ~500-700 words.
  • Add legitimate AggregateRating/Review schema reflecting the 54 Google reviews; add a testimonials section.
  • Build the topic-cluster internal-link model concentrating anchors on non-branded money terms.

Phase 3: Content & Schema Depth Month 2

  • Standardise schema: Breadcrumb on every deep page, FAQ + SoftwareApplication on solutions & accreditation sub-pages.
  • Publish comparison/alternatives pages targeting competitor terms (e.g. 'X alternative', 'best college ERP software India').
  • Add original data/screenshots/client proof to the batch-published guides so they read as maintained.
  • Publish /llms.txt; optionally add Content-Signal lines.

Phase 4: Performance & Monitoring Ongoing

  • Reduce unused JS to pull INP under 200 ms (field).
  • Add Content-Security-Policy header (report-only first).
  • Monitor legacy .php -> new URL consolidation in GSC; resubmit via Indexing API.
  • Track non-branded clicks/impressions and referring-domain count monthly as the north-star KPIs.
